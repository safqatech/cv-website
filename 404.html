About

I build the product, then make sure people can see it.

I am Sabr Botros, a developer on the Libyan Code team. I like work that has to function and still feel considered: a site someone can trust, an application that fits the job, a desktop tool opened every day, a company system that keeps the details straight.

The same care goes into the picture people see. I edit video in Adobe Premiere Pro and manage social media, so a product does not stop at the code. It has a voice, a film, and a place online.

If you need someone who can sit with a problem, write the program, and finish the story around it — that is the work I want.

Software development & creative production studio

Libyan Code is a programming studio in Libya. The team builds websites, mobile and desktop applications, company systems, and the visual work around them — film, identity, and social. The aim is simple: join art and technology, and turn ambition into something you can actually use.
